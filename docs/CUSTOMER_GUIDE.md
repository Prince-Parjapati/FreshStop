# Mandi Khata — Customer guide
**English:** Register → pay ₹3,500 via UPI → enter the UTR → wait for approval (usually minutes) → app unlocks. After that it works fully offline.
**हिन्दी:** रजिस्टर करें → UPI से ₹3,500 भरें → UTR नंबर डालें → अप्रूवल का इंतज़ार करें → ऐप खुल जाएगा। उसके बाद बिना इंटरनेट भी चलता है।
- **Backup regularly** (More → Backup). Your data is only on your phone. Keep the backup password safe — it cannot be recovered.
- New phone? Ask admin to *Reset device*, then restore your backup.
- Sale/Purchase/Receipt/Payment/Kharcha → New Entry tab. Edit, duplicate, share (PDF/WhatsApp/print) from Day Book.
- Deleted entries stay 30 days in Recycle Bin.
